Dojo Zen privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Dojo Zen is a private assistant one family uses to keep up with school news. It isn't offered to the public, and nobody outside the family can create an account.

Gmail

With the account owner's consent, Dojo Zen uses read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly). Google's permission covers the whole mailbox, but Dojo Zen only searches for and opens messages from the school's senders. It can't send, change or delete email.

  • Used for: finding days off, early dismissals, deadlines and events.
  • Kept: each item's date, short title and type, plus the Gmail message ID and the time the message arrived. Message bodies, senders and attachments aren't stored.
  • Shared with: the subject and text of those school messages are sent to Google's Gemini API to pick out the dates.

The results are shown only to the family and to assistants the family has connected. Gmail data isn't sold, isn't used for advertising, and isn't used by Dojo Zen to train AI models.

Dojo Zen's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Other school information

Dojo Zen also reads the family's own ClassDojo feed and messages. These are used for the family's briefings, alerts and questions, and some text and flyers are sent to Google's Gemini API to summarize or read them.

Storage and security

Data is stored in Google Cloud, encrypted in transit and at rest, behind a private access code. Dojo Zen counts how often its features are used, and opens of and clicks in the emails it sends.

Your choices

Gmail access can be withdrawn at any time at Google Account permissions, which stops any further reading. To have stored items deleted, or to ask a question, use the support address shown on Google's sign-in screen.
